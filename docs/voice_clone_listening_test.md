# Snapserve Human Listening Test Protocol (Voice Clone Final Sign-Off)

## 1. Objective
Acoustic embeddings and algorithmic metrics (ECAPA, UTMOS, WER) can miss subtle psychoacoustic artifacts such as uncanny valley inflection, phasing flutter, breathing discontinuity, and micro-clicks. This protocol defines the **human sign-off checklist** required before a custom cloned voice is promoted to live production phone calls.

---

## 2. Test Setup & Panel Specifications
- **Panel Size**: Minimum **10 independent human listeners** (or 5 listeners evaluating 20 A/B stimulus pairs).
- **Listening Environment**: High-fidelity over-ear headphones or calibrated telephone handset simulator (8 kHz / 16 kHz acoustic path).
- **Stimulus Material**:
  - Sample A: Original consented reference recording.
  - Sample B: Cloned voice speech output generated by the production calling pipeline (post-transport, post-AGC).
  - 20 varied conversational sentences from the sales test suite (including the brand name "Snapserve").

---

## 3. Human Evaluation Scorecard (1 - 5 Scale)

| Metric | Score 1 (Unacceptable) | Score 3 (Borderline) | Score 5 (Flawless Human Parity) | Pass Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Speaker Identity** | Distinctly different person | Sounds somewhat like the speaker | Indistinguishable from the speaker | **$\ge 4.0$** |
| **Naturalness** | Completely robotic / monotone | Slightly artificial timbre | Completely warm, expressive human delivery | **$\ge 4.2$** |
| **Pronunciation & Brand** | Distorts "Snapserve" or numbers | Understandable with minor slips | Crisp, perfect phonetics on all brand names | **$\ge 4.5$** |
| **Artifact-Free Quality** | Audible buzzing, clicks, or hiss | Slight metallic trace | Completely pristine audio | **$\ge 4.0$** |
| **Emotional Consistency** | Emotion sounds fake or mismatched | Flat responses to questions | Natural warmth, reacts naturally to humor/surprise | **$\ge 3.8$** |

---

## 4. Sign-Off Signatures & Audit Trail
Before marking `qa_passed = true` on enterprise accounts:
- [ ] Panel of 10 listeners completed the 20-pair A/B scorecard.
- [ ] Median Speaker Identity score $\ge 4.0 / 5.0$.
- [ ] Median Naturalness score $\ge 4.2 / 5.0$.
- [ ] Zero instances of brand slurring on "Snapserve".
- [ ] Reference owner recorded consent statement re-confirmed on file with SHA-256 hash.
